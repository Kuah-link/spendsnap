1. Personal Testing Notice & No-SLA Disclaimer

SpendSnap is a personal, non-commercial Progressive Web App created for testing and personal expense tracking convenience. It is provided strictly "AS IS" without warranties of any kind, express or implied, and no Service Level Agreement (SLA), uptime guarantee, technical support, or data recovery guarantee is provided.

2. Zero Financial Backend (How Your Expense Data Flows)

SpendSnap runs inside your web browser as a static client-side application hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer never receives, views, or stores your financial entries, merchant descriptions, amounts, receipt photos, Google Sheet links, OAuth tokens, or Gemini API keys.

  • Local Device Storage: Your app preferences, temporary offline transaction queue, and personal Gemini API key are stored locally on your own device (localStorage / IndexedDB).
  • Google Account & Drive Data (drive.file scope): When you sign in with Google, your browser communicates directly with Google Drive API and Google Sheets API to create and update the Expense Tracker folder and Expense Ledger spreadsheet inside your own Google Drive. SpendSnap only requests the non-sensitive drive.file scope (plus basic email and profile) and cannot read any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Gemini API Processing: When you scan a receipt image or PDF, your browser sends the compressed file directly to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) using your own Gemini API key with interaction storage disabled (store: false).

3. Basic Active-Tester Roster (Opt-Out Anytime)

Solely to maintain an active beta-tester list during personal testing, when Google Sign-In is active (unless opted out in Settings), SpendSnap may send a lightweight check-in containing only your Google display name, email address, device type (e.g., iOS / Android / Desktop), and app version to a private Google Sheet maintained by the developer, never shared with or sold to third parties.

  • Strictly No Financial Data: Expense amounts, receipt photos, categories, merchants, API keys, and tokens are never included in this check-in.
  • Opt-Out & Removal: You can disable this at any time in Settings → Share basic active-tester status, and you may request immediate deletion of your roster row at any time via the project repository or developer contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

All synced spreadsheets and receipt images remain 100% under your ownership inside your own Google Drive. You may delete your local app data at any time by clearing site storage in your browser, and you may revoke SpendSnap's Google access at any time via Google Account Permissions.
